Your edge. Every match.

Overview

One app. Every edge.

Data Edge brings together everything a serious sports bettor needs — live scores across Football, NBA, MLB, Tennis, and E-sports; real-time odds comparison from multiple bookmakers; AI-powered value signals; expert tips with verified track records; and curated sports news. One product, one place, one unfair advantage.

The design challenge was significant: sports betting data is inherently dense. Match stats, odds tables, bookmaker comparisons, live updates, expert analysis — all of it matters, all at once. Our job was to make that density readable at a glance, navigable with one thumb, and fast enough to actually be useful in the seconds before odds shift.

The Problem

"By the time I've checked three different sites for odds, the value is gone."

Bettors who actually do their research — who look for value, track expert tips, and monitor live data — are being failed by the tools available to them. Everything is scattered. By the time you've found the edge, it's already closed.

01

Fragmented data across platforms

Live scores on one site, odds on another, tips in a Telegram channel, news in a separate app. No bettor should have to context-switch five times to make one decision.

02

Odds that change before you can act

Value windows close in seconds. If your tools require navigating between tabs and refreshing manually, you lose before you start. Speed isn't a nice-to-have — it's the product.

03

No way to know if a tip is worth following

Anyone can claim a winning streak. Without context, reasoning, and a visible history, expert tips are just noise dressed as signal — and bettors have no way to tell the difference.

Expert Analysis

Tips that show
their working.

Anyone can claim to have a winning streak. The Analysis & Tips screen was designed around a single idea: transparency earns trust. Each expert tip surfaces its reasoning, confidence level, and history — not to boast about numbers, but to give users the context they need to make their own informed call.

Analysis & Tips — confidence level and reasoning visible upfront

Purpose

Reduce noise, not add to it

The goal wasn't to surface more tips — it was to surface fewer, better ones. Every design choice in this screen is about helping users filter signal from noise, not overwhelming them with options.

Transparency

Show the reasoning, not just the pick

Confidence levels, sport category, and tip status are always visible. Users can see not just what an expert recommends, but how certain they are and whether past tips have played out — so trust is earned, not assumed.

Control

Filter by sport, status, and outcome

Users can slice tips by sport, filter by pending or settled, and sort by confidence. The screen puts them in control of how they engage — whether they're casually browsing or deeply researching before a match.

Stay Informed

The news section aggregates stories from ESPN, BBC Sport, Sky Sports, and The Athletic — curated by sport and surfaced in a clean, fast-loading feed. Injury news, form guides, and pre-match analysis can shift the value of a line significantly. Data Edge keeps you ahead of it.

What guided every decision

Three principles. One edge.

01

Data density without overwhelm

High-info screens that stay readable at a glance. Dark mode reduces eye strain during evening matches. Yellow highlights only what matters — the best odds, the highest confidence, the live edge.

02

Speed over everything

Bettors make decisions in seconds. Every interaction was optimised for one-handed use, fast scanning, and zero unnecessary taps. If an action takes three taps, that's two too many.

03

Earned trust through transparency

Showing odds from named sources, displaying edge calculations openly, and tracking expert tip history publicly builds a product people actually trust — and come back to every match day.
